Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Car Play Auto Sync for ZLINK ("the app", "we", "us"), published by UtilEdge, handles your information. In short: the app has no account and no server of its own. Your car data is saved on your device, and it only leaves your device for the specific online features listed below.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored in a local database inside the app on your phone or head unit:

  • Vehicles: make, model, year, VIN, registration number, mileage and photos.
  • Maintenance records, activity logs, reminders, fuel fill-ups, expenses and invoices.
  • Documents you add (such as registration or insurance) and their expiry dates.
  • Parking spots you save (location and time).
  • Your profile details, driving licence details and the emergency contacts you add.
  • App settings.

We cannot see or access this data. It is not uploaded or backed up by us. You can delete it at any time from Profile → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Features that use the internet

The following features send data to third-party services, only when you use them:

Photos sent for AI features are used only to produce your result. The app does not keep inspection sessions after you leave them, and we do not store your photos on any server of ours. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

AI results are generated automatically and can be wrong. Inspection with AI is a helpful first look, not a professional vehicle inspection.

3. Location

The app asks for location only while you use it, for the speedometer, saving your parking spot, the map and nearby places. It never requests background location. You can turn location off at any time in Android settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

4. Camera, photos, files and contacts

The app does not request camera, storage or contacts permissions. When you take a photo, pick an image or file, or choose an emergency contact, Android's own camera, photo picker, file picker or contact picker is used, and the app only receives the item you choose.

5. Phone calls

Emergency SOS and Drive Mode open your phone's dialer with the number filled in. The app does not place calls by itself and does not read your call history.

6. Notifications

On Android 13 and later the app asks for notification permission when you set up a reminder, so it can alert you about services, reminders and expiring documents. Notifications are created on your device.

7. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent first using Google's consent message, and you can change your choice later from Profile → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See how Google uses data from partner apps.

8. In-app purchases

If the app offers purchases, payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

9. Children

The app is meant for drivers and vehicle owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections to online services use HTTPS. Please keep your device locked to protect the data on it.

11. Your choices

  • Edit or delete any record in the app at any time.
  • Delete everything with Profile → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Turn off location, notifications or ad personalisation in Android settings.
  • Simply don't use the AI features if you don't want photos to be sent for processing.
