How it works

Set up in under a minute

No sign-up and no account. Add your car and the dashboard is ready.

Install the app

Get Car Play Auto Sync from Google Play on your phone or Android head unit.

Add your car

Type the details, enter the VIN or the registration number, and add a photo.

Log as you go

Save services, fill-ups, bills, documents and parking. Reminders take care of the rest.

Your car data stays on your phone

Vehicles, logs, expenses, documents, parking spots and contacts are saved only on your device. There's no account and no copy on our side. Support

Quick guides

The three things people ask about most.

Add a vehicle

  1. Open the app and tap Add on the Home screen.
  2. Choose manual entry, VIN or registration number.
  3. Fill in the details, add a photo and save.

Save your parking

  1. Tap Parking on Home, or Save Car in Drive Mode.
  2. Allow location while using the app.
  3. Save. Open Parking later to find your car.

Get reminder alerts

  1. Tap Reminders, then Add Reminder.
  2. Set a date, a mileage, or both.
  3. Allow notifications when Android asks.

FAQ

Questions & troubleshooting

Is the app free?
Yes, the app is free to download and use. It shows ads to stay free. AI features have a daily limit per device.
Does it work on my car's head unit?
It is built for Android phones and Android-based head units, including ZLINK units. Drive Mode uses a landscape layout made for wide car screens. Location and place search keep working on units without Google Play Services.
Is this Apple CarPlay or Android Auto?
No. Car Play Auto Sync is an independent car companion app. It is not made by, or connected to, Apple, Google or ZLINK.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up. Your data is saved on your device.
Can I add more than one car?
Yes, add as many vehicles as you like. Each car keeps its own logs, expenses, reminders, documents and parking, and you switch between them from the round photos on Home.
How accurate is Inspection with AI?
AI only checks what your photos show, so it can miss issues or flag something harmless. Use it as a helpful first look, not as a replacement for a mechanic. Clear, well-lit photos give the best results.
The AI result didn't work. What can I do?
Check your internet connection and try a clearer photo of the whole car, taken from the side or a 3/4 angle. If the app says "Incorrect photo", the photo doesn't show the area you picked.
My reminders don't show up.
Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in Android settings, and turn off battery optimisation for it if your phone closes background apps aggressively.
How do I delete my data?
Open Profile → Delete all data. This removes everything from your phone and can't be undone. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
